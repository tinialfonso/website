A bit about me

Industrial designer by training. Program manager by accident. Product manager by choice.

The common thread is curiosity and collaboration, taking something that doesn't quite exist yet and figuring it out with the right people. That's where the interesting work lives.

AI is in the workflow now. It compresses the distance between a question and an answer, and leaves more energy for the parts that actually need human judgment.
